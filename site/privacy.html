Housecall

Privacy policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Developer: Mark Tiddy · Contact: support@marktiddy.co.uk

The short version

Housecall keeps a history of your Apple Home and checks it for problems. That history stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We don't run servers, we can't see your data, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking.

What Housecall reads

With your permission, Housecall reads your Apple Home through Apple's HomeKit:

  • your homes, rooms, accessories, scenes and automations
  • each accessory's state and changes (for example on or off, temperature, battery level, whether it's reachable)
  • for Matter accessories, network diagnostics the accessory shares: Thread or Wi-Fi role and signal strength, restart count and firmware version

On iPhone, if you tap "Find Border Routers" and allow it, Housecall also reads the names and IDs of nearby Thread networks. It never reads or stores network keys.

Where it's stored

  • On your devices, in Housecall's own storage. Local history is kept for 90 days.
  • In your private iCloud database, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac can share history and alerts. Apple's CloudKit stores this in your iCloud account, and we can't access it. Housecall deletes event history from iCloud after 30 days.
  • In a shared container on the device, for widgets: a small summary of your home's status.

What we collect

Nothing. Housecall doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising frameworks, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Housecall Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes payments, and we receive only what Apple shares with every developer: no payment details. Housecall checks your purchase on the device through Apple's StoreKit.

Notifications

Alerts are created on your Mac (or the device you're using) and delivered locally or through your iCloud account. They aren't sent through any server of ours.

Exports

If you export your setup or history, Housecall creates the file on your device and you choose where it goes. What happens to it afterwards depends on where you send it.

Deleting your data

To delete it from a device, delete the Housecall app. To delete it from iCloud, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage (on a Mac: System Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage), choose Housecall, and delete its data.

Children

Housecall isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
